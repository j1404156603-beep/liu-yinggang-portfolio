Enterprise Systems

01 / Medical quality

Hospital Quality Monitoring System

Overview

Case records, data submission, and operation logs are organized within one quality-management chain.

Design Approach

Field grouping reduces input effort, while uncatalogued, archived, submitted, and logged states create an auditable workflow.

02 / Situation awareness

Situation Awareness and Early Warning System

Overview

Monitors healthcare visit data and moves from an overall situation view into individual alerts.

Design Approach

The overview reveals anomalies; detail views support resolution. Maps and trends serve operational judgment.

03 / Collaborative monitoring

Collaborative Big Data Monitoring System

Overview

Public-health information, threat analysis, and warning messages share one collaborative monitoring logic.

Design Approach

Lists consolidate information, statistics explain change, and message details route events to responsible roles.

04 / Epidemiological investigation

Intelligent Epidemiological Investigation System

Overview

Uses investigation tasks and work orders to cover creation, assignment, reporting, and recipient selection.

Design Approach

Questionnaires, work orders, and tasks are organized around how work gets completed rather than as isolated screens.

05 / Pathogen monitoring

Infectious Disease Pathogen Monitoring System

Overview

Pathogen monitoring, case registration, sequencing, and risk assessment support source tracing.

Design Approach

Specialist test results are translated into case clues, spatial risk, and strain comparisons.

06 / Cloud control

Cloud Control Management Platform

Overview

Vehicles, maps, geofences, and dispatch groups are brought into one cloud-control workspace.

Design Approach

The map holds spatial context, groups define dispatch objects, and remote takeover supports human intervention.

07 / Risk control

Vehicle Risk Control Platform

Overview

Vehicle risk status, GIS trajectories, warning handling, and reports form an operational loop.

Design Approach

Metrics show trends, maps show individual objects, warnings show actions, and reports retain decision-relevant dimensions.
