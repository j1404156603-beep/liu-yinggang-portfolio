Operational Dashboards for Complex Systems

This collection brings together visualization work for operational monitoring, dispatch, and risk warning. Across different domains, the challenge is consistent: organize dense information within limited screen space so users can understand the whole, locate anomalies, and move into specific objects and tasks. My scope varied by project and included information architecture, interface and visual design, design standards, and engineering delivery.
